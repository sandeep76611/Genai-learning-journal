# Introduction to AI
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science focused on creating machines that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

## Applications in Healthcare
AI helps doctors by analyzing medical images, predicting patient outcomes, and providing personalized treatment recommendations.

## Applications in Finance
AI detects fraudulent transactions, improves customer service via chatbots, and helps with credit risk analysis.

## Challenges
AI raises ethical issues, privacy concerns, and requires careful handling of sensitive data.
